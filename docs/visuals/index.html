When someone leaves, what leaves with them?

Inheritance maps who owns which code, who made which decisions, and who works with whom. Then it shows what a departure takes away, and who already carries it.

Real public records from Kubernetes, one of the largest engineering organizations in open source. When a maintainer steps away, their name moves to an emeritus list. That is a real history of people leaving.

Who do I ask?

Ownership is inherited up the folder tree

To find who approves a file, climb from its folder to the nearest OWNERS file that names approvers. Some OWNERS files name only reviewers, so the climb continues along the tree's parent links.
